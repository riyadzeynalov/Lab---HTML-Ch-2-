Sumgait, Azerbaijan

Location

This town is located in Azerbaijan, about 20 miles away from the capital Baku

About

Weather

Summers are very hot and humid, while winters are cold, with some snow.

Population

As a second largest city in Azerbaijan after capital Baku, Sumgait has a population of approximately 427,000 people.

Sports

Soccer is the most popular sport in Sumgait.

Things to Do

  • Visit the History Museum
  • Go swimming at the beach
  • Take a walk along the Caspian Sea coast
  • Enjoy the local food and cuisine
